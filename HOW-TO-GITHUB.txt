GITHUB – what to upload
=======================
Upload ALL files in this folder EXCEPT this HOW-TO file:
  index.html   (the whole app)
  robots.txt   (tells Google what to skip)
  sitemap.xml  (tells Google your page address)
  README.md
  .gitignore   (keeps secret keys out of GitHub; if your phone hides it, skip it and add later from a computer)

Steps
1. github.com > "+" > New repository. Name: ghana-ncc. Choose Private. Create.
2. Tap "uploading an existing file".
3. Select the files above. Tap Commit changes.

Do NOT upload any keys or passwords to GitHub.
After the upload, go to folder 3-VERCEL.
